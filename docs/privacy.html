Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Short version: the Bot does not read your messages. It saves each server’s settings and briefly keeps timestamps in memory to spot raids and spam.

What we store

For each server the Bot is in, we save its configuration to disk:

  • Server ID
  • The log channel ID, if one is set with /logchannel
  • IDs of roles exempted with /whitelist
  • Numeric settings and the lockdown action chosen with /config

What we process temporarily

While the Bot is running, it keeps the following in memory only. It is discarded within seconds, or when the Bot restarts:

  • Times at which members joined a server, to detect join floods
  • Times at which members sent messages, with their user and server IDs, to detect spam
  • The number of user and role mentions in a message
  • Account creation date of members who join during a lockdown
  • Whether a server is currently in lockdown

What we don’t collect

  • Message content — the Bot does not request the Message Content intent
  • Direct messages
  • Email addresses, IP addresses, or payment information

Alerts and logs

When the Bot acts, it posts an alert to the server’s configured log channel. The alert includes the member’s username, user ID, the action taken, the reason, and for joins, account age. Those messages are stored by Discord and are controlled by that server’s administrators. The Bot also writes short operational logs, including server names and the same alert text, for troubleshooting.

How we use data

Only to run the Bot’s protection features and to troubleshoot problems. We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Server settings are kept until you ask us to delete them. Removing the Bot from your server stops all further processing. To have a server’s saved settings deleted, contact us from an account with administrator rights on that server.

Third parties

The Bot runs on Discord, and your use of Discord is covered by Discord’s Privacy Policy.

Children

The Bot is not directed at children below the minimum age required to use Discord.
