3. The service

The Bot provides automated anti-raid protection for Discord servers, including:

  • Join-flood detection — when many members join within a short window, the Bot enables a lockdown and kicks or times out new joiners until the lockdown ends.
  • Spam and mass-mention detection — members who send messages too quickly or mention too many users or roles are timed out and their recent messages are deleted.
  • Alerts and admin commands — actions are reported to a log channel, and administrators can configure the Bot with slash commands such as /status, /lockdown, /unlock, /config, /logchannel and /whitelist.

We may add, change, or remove features at any time.
